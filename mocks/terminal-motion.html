03

Streaming typewriter

Today: chunks land whole, the tblink cursor is the only thing alive — which actually highlights that the text is static. Fix (mirrors frontend/src/hooks/useSmooth.ts): two layers — bursty network fills a buffer (5-60 char chunks, 30-400ms gaps), a 10ms drain emits 1-4 chars/tick into the DOM. Drain accelerates (1→2→3→4 chars) when buffer falls behind by 20/40/80. Each emitted batch fades 0→1 in 180ms. Same algorithm Vercel AI SDK's smoothStream ships and ChatGPT/Claude use. Press stream.
