Every flat map of the Earth is a lie, but some lies are useful. The fundamental constraint is geometric, not technical.
Three families of projections
Conformal projections preserve angles. Equal-area preserve area. Compromise projections minimise the worst distortion. The choice depends on whether you are navigating, mapping thematic data, or just illustrating.

能简单解释一下 Winkel Tripel 为什么比 Robinson 更准确吗？ Compare on shape distortion.
> michi
Winkel Tripel minimises three errors at once — area, direction, and distance — using an averaging trick over a Mollweide and an equirectangular projection. Robinson was tuned by eye, not by formula, so its trade-offs aren't reproducible mathematically.
